# Business Goals and Objectives

Status: Draft
Parent: [[solution-overview]]
Source: European CRM Architecture Review 2.pdf

## Primary Goals

1. **Unified Customer View** — Single source of truth for customer data across all four European offices
2. **Regulatory Compliance** — Meet GDPR and regional data protection requirements
3. **Process Standardisation** — Consistent CRM workflows across France, Germany, Spain, Poland
4. **Operational Efficiency** — Reduce manual processes and improve data quality
5. **Cross-Sell Enablement** — Enable cross-border and cross-service-line collaboration
6. **Scalable Reporting** — Automate transaction reporting as deal volume grows

## MVP Success Criteria

The MVP answers:
- "Who is this client to Knight Frank across every service line, every country?"
- "What does our EU pipeline look like?"
- "How do we run CM deals?"

## Future Phase Goals

- How do we run every service line, in every country?
- Full Customer Insights & Journeys for marketing
- Centralised EU ERP for finance
- AI-powered matching and copilot capabilities
