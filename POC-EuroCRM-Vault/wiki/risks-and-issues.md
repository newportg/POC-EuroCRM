## Risk of Inaction

The register below captures the risks of **not** proceeding with the EuroCRM programme — i.e. the cost of maintaining the status quo. Likelihood/impact ratings are provisional and to be confirmed with stakeholders during the requirements phase.

## Risk Register

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
| ---- | ---------- | ------ | ---------- | ----- |
| Missed cross-border opportunities — relationship and client/market intelligence sits locally and isn't visible across Europe; where it is, unlocking it takes tremendous manual effort | Medium | High | Deliver a single, trusted view of clients, contacts, properties and opportunities across the region | TBD |
| No institutionalised client knowledge — knowledge sits with individuals in Excel trackers, mailboxes, and black books | High | High | Centralise fragmented data into a common client/contact/property foundation | TBD |
| Employee frustration and attrition — HR surveys flag frustration with the current technology landscape and manual effort; modernising improves efficiency, satisfaction, and talent retention | High | Medium | Streamline lead and deal management and reduce manual reporting processes | TBD |
| Excessive data management and reporting effort — reconciling information and assembling regional reports consumes significant time and effort | High | Medium | Reduce reliance on manual reporting; provide a foundation for standardised reporting | TBD |
| Missed AI / advanced analytics value — fragmented data blocks leveraging AI and advanced analytics | Medium | High | Establish a clean, structured data foundation enabling reporting, analytics and future AI-driven insights | TBD |
