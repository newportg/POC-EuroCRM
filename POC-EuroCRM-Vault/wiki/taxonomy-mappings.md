# Taxonomy Mappings

## Client Industry Taxonomy — SIC to KF Mapping

SIC gives the common backbone; KF sectors describe how we actually serve clients.

**The Standard:** Start from SIC — the recognised statistical classification behind UK and European company records.

**The Translation:** Map to how we advise — each SIC division is translated into a KF client industry sector and sub-segment, in the language our teams use with clients.

**The Bridge:** Close the modern gaps — client types SIC could not anticipate, such as sovereign wealth funds, family offices, SPVs, REITs and PropTech, are mapped explicitly.

### Strategic Sectors

| Strategic KF Client Sector | SIC Mapping | Significance | Notes |
| -------------------------- | ----------- | ------------ | ----- |
| Private Equity & Funds | 64.2 / 64.3 | Core | Major investment clients across all asset classes |
| Sovereign Wealth | 64.3 | Core | Not explicit in SIC — mapped via Other Financial Services |
| Banking & Financial Services | 64.1 / 66 | Core | Major occupier and investor clients |
| Insurance & Pensions | 65 | Core | Direct RE investors; large portfolio holders |
| Real Estate | 68 | Core | REITs, property companies, asset managers, developers |
| Real Estate Development | 41 | Core | House builders, commercial developers |
| Technology | 62 / 63 | Core+ | Growing occupier demand; data centre and campus clients |
| Life Sciences & Pharmaceuticals | 21 / 72 | Emerging | Specialist lab, campus and manufacturing requirements |
| Logistics & Distribution | 52 | Core+ | 3PL operators, warehouse and fulfilment clients |

### SIC Gaps

Modern client types not explicitly covered by SIC:

| Modern Client Type | KF Enterprise Mapping | Gap Description |
| ------------------ | --------------------- | --------------- |
| Sovereign Wealth Funds | Financial Services & Investment | SIC bundles all investment funds under 64.3 |
| Family Offices | UHNW & Private Individuals | No specific code; split between financial services and household employers |
| Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs) | Financial Services & Investment | Grouped under holding companies |
| REITs | Real Estate | No distinction between listed REITs and operating property companies |
| PropTech | Technology | Bundled under IT consultancy |
| Co-Working Operators | Real Estate | Classified as real estate; no distinction from traditional landlords |
| Infrastructure Funds | Financial Services & Investment | No distinction from other collective investment vehicles |
| Private Equity (RE-focused) | Financial Services & Investment | No distinction between RE-focused PE and general PE |
| Pension Fund Direct RE | Insurance & Pensions | No distinction between direct RE investment and general pension activity |

## Asset Class Taxonomy — HILUCS to KF Mapping

HILUCS gives the common backbone for Knight Frank asset classes.

**The Standard:** Start from HILUCS — the European INSPIRE land-use classification gives every market one shared way to describe what a building or parcel of land is used for.

**The Translation:** Map to how we trade — every HILUCS class is translated into the Knight Frank enterprise sector and the asset classes our teams transact.

**The Bridge:** Close the modern gaps — classes HILUCS could not anticipate, such as data centres, BTR, PBSA and self storage, are bridged to the nearest standard class.

### Strategic Sectors

| Strategic KF Sector | HILUCS Mapping | Significance | Notes |
| ------------------- | -------------- | ------------ | ----- |
| Office | 3.2.2 | Core | Traditional institutional asset class |
| Retail | 3.1.1 | Core | Evolving with omnichannel strategies |
| Industrial | 2.1 / 2.2 / 2.3 | Core | Manufacturing and production facilities |
| Logistics | 4.2 | Core+ | High growth sector driven by e-commerce |
| Data Centres | 3.2.3 | Emerging | Not explicit in HILUCS — mapped via ICT Services |
| Life Sciences | 3.2.2 | Emerging | Not explicit in HILUCS — mapped via Professional Services |
| Healthcare | 3.3.3 | Core | Defensive sector with demographic tailwinds |
| Living | 5.x | Core+ | Umbrella for all residential-related sectors |
| PBSA | 5.3 | Emerging | Not explicit in HILUCS — mapped via Other Residential |

### HILUCS Gaps

Modern asset classes not explicitly covered by HILUCS:

| Modern Asset Class | Enterprise Mapping | Gap Description |
| ------------------ | ------------------ | --------------- |
| Data Centres | Digital Infrastructure | HILUCS treats as generic ICT services |
| Life Sciences | Life Sciences | Bundled under professional services |
| Build to Rent (BTR) | Build to Rent (BTR) | No distinction from owner-occupied residential |
| PBSA | PBSA | Classified as 'Other Residential' |
| Self Storage | Self Storage | Bundled with logistics |
| Senior Living | Senior Living | Split between Residential and Healthcare |
| Co-Living | Co-Living | No recognition of shared living concepts |
| Multifamily | Multifamily | No distinction from general residential |
| Cold Storage | Cold Storage | Bundled with general logistics |
